# AutoLedger Roadmap v1

## Product Development Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of AutoLedger from a policy-driven AI accounting operator into a fully autonomous finance operations platform.

---

# Phase 1: Foundation & Controls

## Objective

Establish the security, policy, approval, and audit foundations required for safe autonomous accounting operations.

### Core Deliverables

#### User Authentication

* User registration
* Login and session management
* Secure authentication workflows

#### Multi-Company Management

* Company-level isolation
* Multi-company ownership support
* Company context switching

#### Autonomy Engine

Operational modes:

* Autonomous
* Semi-Autonomous
* Manual

#### Policy Framework

* Hard-stop approval rules
* Company-specific policies
* Approval thresholds
* Risk controls

#### Inbox Lifecycle Engine

Workflow states:

1. New
2. Needs Review
3. Awaiting Approval
4. Approved
5. Posted
6. Blocked
7. Closed

#### Audit Logging

* User actions
* AI decisions
* Approval history
* System events

### Success Criteria

* Secure access controls
* Reliable policy enforcement
* Complete audit traceability

# Long-Term Vision

AutoLedger evolves from an accounting automation tool into a fully autonomous finance operations platform capable of:

* Reading
* Understanding
* Communicating
* Approving
* Executing
* Reporting

while maintaining complete policy compliance, transparency, and auditability.

The ultimate goal is to provide every business with an AI-powered accounting operator capable of handling day-to-day finance operations safely and intelligently.
