# AutoLedger AI

## AI-Powered Accounting Assistant

AutoLedger AI is an intelligent accounting automation platform designed to reduce manual bookkeeping work by leveraging Artificial Intelligence, document processing, voice interactions, and workflow automation.

The platform aims to transform invoices, receipts, emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, and voice conversations into structured accounting workflows and ERP-ready transactions.

---

## Vision

Traditional accounting processes are heavily dependent on manual data entry and repetitive administrative work.

AutoLedger AI is being designed to help businesses:

- Automate bookkeeping workflows
- Process invoices and receipts
- Extract financial data from documents
- Generate accounting entries
- Support voice-based accounting operations
- Integrate with ERP and accounting platforms
- Improve financial reporting efficiency
